All-Time Favourites

★

A Thousand Splendid Suns by Khaled Hosseini

★★★★★

This broke me in every possible way. As a muslim women who is praying for afghan women everyday, this was a book I never want to read again, but I believe everyone should read once in their life.

Pride And Prejudice by Jane Austen

★★★★★

A classic for a reason. It was very gentle, and one of the first classic romances I read as a child and I still go back to it everytime.

La Petite Fadette by George Sand

★★★★★

Also one of the classics I read as a child and that I always go back to. It is such a beautiful story that touched my heart in all the perfect ways.

The Love Hypothesis

★★★★★

I really loved Olive and Adam's characters and dynamic throughout the book. And the revelation that he had liked her for a long time? Oh, I was feral!

Previous Reads

Paladin's Grace by T. Kingfisher (Saint of Steel #1)

★★★★☆

We love a giant scary man who is secretely a softie obssessed with knitting socks. And the murder mystery? Chef's kiss.

Paladin's Strength by T. Kingfisher (Saint of Steel #2)

★★★★☆

I LOVE when the female lead is presented to be as tall and big as the main lead. The werebears storyline was so unique and amazing! And the mystery continuing from the first book was incredibly well-done.

Paladin's Hope by T. Kingfisher (Saint of Steel #3)

★★★☆☆

LGBT representation in a fantasy setting. Loved the two main leads, though the mystery in this one was less interesting in my opinion.

Paladin's Faith by T. Kingfisher (Saint of Steel #4)

★★★⯪☆

Marguerite and Shane were my favourite side characters throughout the series, so I was definitely set to enjoy a story involving the both of them!

Deep end by Ali Hazelwood

★★★⯪☆

The second book I read by Ali Hazelwood, and that was very enjoyable. I like it when books break the stereotypes people have of different kinks, especially ones revolving around BDSM. The romance was cute, and I don't care if they forgave her, I still hate Pen.

Cinder by Marissa Meyer

★★★★☆

Cinderella meets robot and technology? As an IT student and an avid fairytale lover, I ate the hell out of this book.

It ends With Us

★★☆☆☆

I have always had mixed feelings about this book. As a reader, it was the first english book I read after reading in french for so long, and it made me love read in english. But a lot of things could have been done better from a wrier perspective.
