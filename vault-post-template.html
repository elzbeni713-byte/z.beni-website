Gentle Scent

A quiet story of grief, fragrance, healing, and an unexpected kind of love.

By Z. Beni Fiction Novel
STORY COVER

Genevieve has always found comfort in fragrance. As an orphan and a young perfumer's apprentice, she has built a quiet life around delicate scents, careful work, and the memories carried by flowers.

Her days are simple. There are bottles to prepare, flowers to sort, customers to please, and countless little details that demand her attention. It is a life she has learned not to ask too much from.

Then Derek Loewen arrives in her life.

Once a guard in the royal army, Derek now serves the Duke of Corlett. He is a man who carries his past quietly, rarely allowing anyone close enough to ask about the things he has lost.

Their paths were never supposed to cross in any meaningful way. Yet somehow, between quiet conversations and the gentle scent of orange blossom, something begins to change.

Neither of them expects love to find them there.

Sometimes healing does not arrive dramatically.

Sometimes it is found in the smallest things: a familiar fragrance, a warm room, someone's quiet presence, or the feeling that you no longer have to carry everything alone.

And sometimes, after spending years simply surviving, peace begins to feel a little like love.

Some things are not meant to be forgotten.
They are meant to become something softer.
