A little introduction of me

I'm Z. Beni!

My name is Zineb, and I'm a 22-year-old woman from Morocco, who has loved reading since I was a child. Z. Beni is my pen name, inspired from my real name Zineb, which means in arabic "ornament," "adornment," or "fragrant flower".

As a proud North African, I speak Arabic, French and English fluently and read books in the latter two. But even if it is not my mother tongue, English is the language of my heart which inspires me and made me aspire to be an English author.

I am currently in my final year of Computer Science Engineering, while also completing my Bachelor's degree in English Literature. Two very opposite majors that make me who I am today.

I created this space to document my journey, share my thoughts through the process of writing, and hopefully make another writer feel a little less alone in the overwhelming process of writing.
